Privacy & Credits

Your privacy, and open-source acknowledgements.

This site is a professional portfolio showcasing graphic design, vectorization, video editing, and web development. It measures visits only when you consent, and only collects personal data when you submit a project enquiry.

Privacy Notice

In short: No third-party advertisements, no behavioural trackers, and no tracking pixels. Anonymous visit metrics are processed via Google Analytics only if you click “Allow” on the consent banner. Until you consent, no analytics cookies are set.

Who is responsible

Haroon Mughal GFX. For any questions regarding your information or project inquiries, you can reach out directly via email at designto@haroonmughalgfx.online.

What this site stores in your browser

This portfolio utilizes standard browser local storage (`localStorage`) for two non-identifying preferences:

  • haroon-mode: Stores your light or dark visual theme selection.
  • haroon-analytics: Stores your choice (“yes” or “no”) regarding analytics measurement.

Neither preference tracks personal browsing activity across websites, and both can be cleared anytime in your browser settings.

Analytics measurement

If you choose “Allow” on our banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC), which sets cookies (with names beginning with _ga) to measure pageviews, general device type, and referral traffic. This information is used strictly to understand which work resonates with visitors. IP addresses are anonymized. If you choose “No thanks”, Google Analytics is never loaded.

Project enquiries & contact

When you use the contact form on this site, your message is pre-formatted and sent via your email client directly to designto@haroonmughalgfx.online. We collect your name, email, optional phone number, and project specifications solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential collaboration. We never sell, lease, or share your contact details with third parties.

Hosting and security

This website is hosted statically via GitHub Pages and served over HTTPS encrypted connections. Edge request logs are processed by the hosting provider solely to deliver web assets and protect against automated denial-of-service abuse.

Your privacy rights
